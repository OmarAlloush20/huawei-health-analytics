Huawei Health Analytics

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy explains how Huawei Health Analytics, an Android wellness and fitness analytics application, handles information.

Wellness use only. The app is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency alerts.

Health data and authorization

The app accesses HUAWEI Health Service Kit data only after you take an explicit authorization action and grant the requested access. It does not request Health Service Kit write scopes and does not modify your HUAWEI Health records.

When a corresponding feature, approved Health Service Kit scope, and provider integration are available—and only when you authorize that access—the app may read:

  • steps and calories;
  • medium- and high-intensity activity;
  • activity and workout records;
  • heart rate and resting heart rate;
  • stress;
  • sleep;
  • blood oxygen saturation (SpO₂);
  • heart-health and heart-rate-variability-related data; and
  • authorized historical data for those categories.

Current implementation note: the Huawei provider in the current source repository requests and reads authorized step data only. The other categories above describe access the app may use after their scopes are approved and their provider integrations are implemented; they are not currently queried by this version.

How health data is used

Authorized data may be used on your device to provide daily summaries, personal baselines, Recovery scoring, trends, and explainable wellness insights. Results are descriptive and may be incomplete or inaccurate when source data is unavailable.

Local storage and network services

Health data processed by the app is stored in its local database on your device. The app currently has no custom user account or application backend for storing health data. It does not send health data to an app-operated server.

HUAWEI Health and Health Service Kit are separate Huawei services and may communicate with Huawei systems under Huawei's terms and privacy practices. Data already held by Huawei is not part of the app's local database.

The app does not currently include an analytics or behavioral-tracking service. It does not use a remote push-notification system; current reminders are scheduled locally by your device. Notification content does not include health measurements.

The local database is not separately encrypted by the app and relies on your device's security controls. Android backup is intentionally disabled for the app's health data.

Your choices and deletion

  • You can decline HUAWEI Health authorization and continue using any features that do not require it.
  • You can revoke authorization through the relevant Huawei Health, Health Service Kit, or Android system controls.
  • The app provides local health-data deletion and development reset functionality.
  • Clearing app data or uninstalling the app removes its local database, subject to normal Android platform behavior.

Deleting local app data does not delete original records stored separately by Huawei or HUAWEI Health. If you later authorize access again, historical data may be synchronized again where the HUAWEI API, approved scopes, and app implementation permit it.

Compact daily summaries can remain locally until you delete/reset the data or uninstall the app. Detailed local records may also be removed by the app's local retention process.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The effective date at the top will be revised when an updated policy is published.
